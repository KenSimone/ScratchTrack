Quick Start

Glossary - If you read anything, read this.
Term
 Definition
Account
 An account holds money or debt. Checking, savings, credit card, and 'under you mattress' are all valid account types in ScratchTrack.
Expense
 Expenses are any kind of outflow of money.
Envelope
 An envelope is a place to stash money for upcoming expenses. You'll create envelopes for different categories of expenses. For instance, you might have envelopes titled 'groceries','rent', 'eating out', etcetera. In traditional envelope budgeting, you'd have real cash-stuffed envelopes with category names written on them. In ScratchTrack envelopes are figurative.
Folder
 Envelopes belong to a folder. I have three folders in my own workflow. The first two envelopes align with paychecks and are titled "15th" and "31st", which are the dates I'm paid by my employer. These folders contain envelopes that I contribute to each month.The third folder is titled "out of band" and contains envelopes that I don't fill ony a regular basis.
Transaction
 A record of a money moving
